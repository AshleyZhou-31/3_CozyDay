Integrated Feature:
CozyDay now includes a login-protected local RAG Planning Assistant at
/planitems/ai-rag-planner/. The Django form validates a planning question, embeds it
with sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2, retrieves the top three hybrid chunks by
cosine similarity, and generates a grounded response with Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct.
The result page displays the answer, retrieved sources, similarity scores, warnings and
per-stage latency. A relevance gate refuses low-relevance questions, and output
validation removes unfinished token-limit tails.
